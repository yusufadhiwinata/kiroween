HAUNTED ADVENTURE — ORIGINAL AUDIO STARTER PACK
=================================================

Contents
--------
Music/     8 original synthesized page themes (24 seconds each)
Ambience/  8 original synthesized ambience beds (16 seconds each)

All files are WAV PCM, 44.1 kHz, 16-bit, mono.
Music and ambience are loop-oriented. Crossfade at the loop boundary if your engine exposes a small seam.

Mixing notes
------------
- Start music around -18 to -14 dBFS in-game, ambience around -30 to -24 dBFS.
- Keep one-shot SFX short and lower than dialogue/UI feedback.
- Duck ambience slightly when important SFX plays.
- Use separate volume controls for Music, Ambience, and SFX.
- These are original synthesized placeholder/game-ready assets, not field recordings or orchestral performances.
- You may use, modify, and bundle these generated files in your own app/game. Do not claim they are recordings of real wildlife or real-world environments.
